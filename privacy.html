ForsakeAI

Privacy

Last updated October 17, 2026

ForsakeAI has no servers of its own and no accounts. It collects nothing about you. This page lists every place your text or settings can end up.

Your text

  • Cleanup and on-device rewriting run inside your browser. The text is not sent anywhere.
  • With your own API key, the passages being rewritten go straight from your browser to the provider you chose (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, OpenRouter, Groq, Mistral, or a server on your own machine). That provider's privacy policy applies to that request. ForsakeAI never sees it.
  • Google Gemini's free tier is the suggested way to start. On it, Google may use what you send to improve its products, and human reviewers may read it (Gemini API terms). With billing turned on, or in the EEA, Switzerland and the UK, Google doesn't use it that way. Don't send confidential text on the free tier.

What is stored in your browser

These stay on your device. Clearing site data for this page removes all of them.

  • Your last 20 drafts and their progress (IndexedDB), so an interrupted rewrite can resume.
  • API keys, only if you tick "Remember this key". They are stored as plain text unless you add a passphrase, which encrypts them with AES-GCM.
  • Your writing sample for voice matching, if you paste one.
  • Settings: theme, spelling, provider and model choice, and whether you've already seen the one-time support note.
  • Downloaded on-device models (Cache Storage).

Third parties you connect to

  • GitHub Pages hosts this site and, like any web host, receives your IP address when the page loads. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Hugging Face and GitHub serve the on-device model files when you choose on-device rewriting or a local server, including a 129 MB model that checks rewrites kept your meaning, and a 438 MB AI-text detector if you run the AI check. They receive your IP address for that download. The AI check runs in your browser; your text is not sent anywhere.

What ForsakeAI does not do

  • No analytics, tracking pixels, ads or third-party scripts.
  • No cookies.
  • No account, email address or payment details.

Your responsibility

ForsakeAI edits writing. It does not guarantee how any AI detector will score a text. If your school or employer has rules about AI tools, you are responsible for following them.
